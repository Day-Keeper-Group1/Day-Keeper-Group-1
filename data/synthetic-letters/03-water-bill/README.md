# 03 · Water bill

Sample `SYN-0003`, printed in small type at the bottom right of each page. Two pages. A quarterly water bill from a fictional water company, Calderfield Water, to Mrs M A Wilson, issued on 21 October 2026. The answer key is [`ground-truth.json`](ground-truth.json).

## Where it comes from

This bill follows an official annotated example document. Its layout is copied from the two-page sample bill on Greater Western Water's "How to read your water bill" page. Greater Western Water is one of the water companies owned by the Victorian government. The sample is a made-up bill for a made-up customer, Sam Sample, with notes that explain each block. No real customer's bill was used. The labels follow that one sample and are not mixed with labels from other water companies. The amounts printed on the sample were not used.

The bill was built up from that sample, two other sample bills, Victorian regulation, the prices the regulator approved for that company, and the state's concession rules:

- **How to read your water bill** (Greater Western Water). The annotated sample bill, as two page images and a PDF with the company's notes. It gave the layout of page 1: the postal barcode above the address, the three boxes for "Account number", "Amount to pay" and "Pay by", the five summary lines from "Previous bill" to "Total charges", the "Having trouble paying your bill?" box, the usage panel with a five-bar chart, and the five payment blocks with the ABN on the right. It gave the layout of page 2: the "Usage and charges" tables, the "(E)" note for an estimated meter reading, the privacy note naming the Privacy and Data Protection Act 2014 (Vic), and the "Your charges explained" and "We're here to help" column. Its notes also say that service charges are billed in advance. That is why the network charges on page 2 cover 1 October to 31 December 2026, and the water use covers 1 July to 30 September 2026. <https://www.gww.com.au/accounts-billing/prices-charges/understanding-your-bill>
- **How to read my bill** (Yarra Valley Water). A second annotated sample bill, front and back. Its layout was not used. It gave the way the usage step is written in litres per day, and the "1 Kilolitre (kL) = 1000 Litres (L)" line. It also gave two facts about the concession. The concession does not cover the waterways and parks charges. Each bill's concession is one quarter of the yearly cap. <https://www.yvw.com.au/help-advice/watercare/understand-my-bill/how-read-my-bill>
- **Understanding your bill** (South East Water). A third sample bill. Its layout was not used. None of the three sample bills prints a separate GST line, so this bill does not have one. <https://southeastwater.com.au/accounts-and-billing/manage-account/your-bill/>
- **Water Industry Standard: Urban Customer Service, version 5** (Essential Services Commission, Victoria). The rules for Victorian water bills, in force from 1 July 2026. Clause 6.3 lists the 17 things a bill must show. These include the issue date, the account number, the meter read date and a note when the reading is estimated, the average daily use, the amount to pay, the pay-by date, the payment methods, help for customers who have trouble paying, a 24 hour faults line, help in other languages, and the concessions a customer can get. Clause 6.6 requires the usage chart, including the same time last year. Clause 7.1 lists the payment methods, including deductions from welfare payments. Clause 15.5(b) says a concession card holder cannot be charged interest, so the bill has no interest notice. <https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/Water%20Industry%20Standard%20-%20Urban%20Customer%20Service%20version%205.pdf>
- **Greater Western Water 2026 to 27 tariff schedule** (Essential Services Commission). The maximum prices the regulator approved for that company. It has one column for prices from 1 July 2025 and one column for prices from 1 July 2026. The water was used in July to September 2026, so the usage rate of \$3.8954 per kL comes from the 1 July 2026 column. The network charges are for October to December 2026, so the yearly water network fee of \$242.34 and the yearly sewerage network fee of \$325.98 come from the 1 July 2026 column. The schedule also shows that homes pay no sewage disposal charge per kilolitre, and that there are two usage steps. The bill follows both. <https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/Greater%20Western%20Water%20-%202026-27%20tariff%20schedule.pdf>
- **Greater Western Water 2025 to 26 maximum prices schedule** (Essential Services Commission). The same table from the year before. Its prices from 1 July 2025 match the 2026 to 27 schedule exactly. This checks that the table was read correctly; no price from it is printed on this bill. <https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2025-26-Tariff-Approvals-Greater-Western-Water-Maximum-Prices-Schedule-20250611.pdf>
- **Victorian concessions: a guide to discounts and services, September 2025** (Department of Families, Fairness and Housing, Victoria). The 50 percent water and sewerage concession. It also shows that no concession covers the waterways and parks charges. <https://services.dffh.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-09/Victorian%20concessions%20-%20guide%20to%20discounts%20and%20services-september-2025.pdf>
- **Water and Sewerage Concession: information for cardholders** (Department of Families, Fairness and Housing, Victoria). It confirmed that the \$372.10 yearly cap on the department's pages is the figure for 2025 to 26. <https://services.dffh.vic.gov.au/water-and-sewerage-concession-information-cardholders-word>
- **Apply for a concession** (South East Water). The 2026 to 27 yearly concession cap of \$381.40 for a home with both water and sewerage. One quarter of it is the \$95.35 "Pensioner concession" on page 2. <https://southeastwater.com.au/accounts-and-billing/financial-assistance/apply-for-a-concession/>
- **Waterways and Drainage Charge** (Melbourne Water). The 2026 to 27 yearly charge for a home, \$130.56. One quarter of it is the \$32.64 "Waterways" charge on page 2. The page names the law behind the charge, section 259 of the Water Act 1989 (Vic). <https://www.melbournewater.com.au/services/prices-and-charges/waterways-and-drainage-charge>
- **Parks charge** (Parks Victoria). The water company collects the parks charge under section 143 of the Water Industry Act 1994 (Vic). The charge is worked out from the property's net annual value. <https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/about-us/parks-charge>
- **Residential prices and charges 2026 to 27** (South East Water). The quarterly and yearly amounts for the waterways and parks charges in 2026 to 27. <https://southeastwater.com.au/accounts-and-billing/prices-and-charges/prices-and-charges-residential/>
- **Centrepay Procedural Guide, version 6** (Services Australia). The rules for showing a welfare deduction option on a bill. The option must sit inside the section that lists the ways to pay. It must sit next to the other payment methods and have the same weight as them. The paystead block on page 1 follows these two rules. It is the last of the five blocks under "Payment options", with the same size and style as the other four. <https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/centrepay-procedural-guide>
- **GSTR 2000/25: GST-free supplies of water, sewerage and sewerage-like services** (Australian Taxation Office). Water and sewerage services are GST-free under sections 38-285 and 38-290 of the GST Act. This is why the bill shows no GST. <https://www8.austlii.edu.au/au/other/rulings/ato/ATOGSTR/2000/GSTR200025.html>

Made up, not taken from any source: the names Calderfield Water, Calderfield Catchment Board, Calderfield Reserves Board, Kelsworth Post, Public Payments Office and paystead, the ABN, the account, invoice and meter numbers, every biller code, payment reference, phone number, website and email address, the customer and her address, her water use, meter readings and chart figures, the previous bill and her last payment, the logo and the colours. The meter number keeps the sample's pattern of three letters and six digits, with different letters. The postal barcode above the address looks like a real one but does not encode anything. No real payment network name is printed. The bank block is headed "Phone and internet banking", and the post office block uses the made-up Kelsworth Post with a "Payment code". The post office payment code was picked from a list of made-up codes that were each checked and found unused. The welfare deduction block follows the Centrepay guide's rules on where it sits, but its names and its reference number are made up. The address is printed on three lines with the suburb in capitals, which follows the dataset's own character profile. The concession sentence under the chart on page 1 was written for this bill from clause 6.3(o) of the standard. The property's net annual value of \$39,100 is worked out from a made-up property value. Some calculations are choices made for this bill, because no source says how to do them. The yearly network fees are spread by days, 92 out of 365. The parks rate of 0.001348 is one quarter of the published yearly rate. The concession uses the 2026 to 27 cap; the issue date and both periods fall in that financial year. The concession is worked out on the water and sewerage charges only. Four official sources point this way, but no law lists the charges it covers.

## The six fields

### document_type

**Water bill.** Page 1, top left, the logo reads "Calderfield Water". The details under the "Account number" box start with "Tax invoice". The chart in the middle of page 1 is headed "Your household water usage". Page 2 opens with "Usage and charges" and then "Your water usage". The answer key stores it as `water_bill`. This field is free text and the experiments do not score it.

### issuer

**Calderfield Water.** Page 1, top left, the logo reads "Calderfield Water" on two lines. The same name is printed at the right end of the "Payment options" heading ("Calderfield Water ABN 33 321 818 706"), in the paystead payment block ("Calderfield Water reference:"), and at the start of the privacy note on page 2. It is the company sending the bill and asking to be paid.

### action_required

**Pay Calderfield Water.** Page 1, the dark box "Amount to pay" in the middle of the three boxes under the address, the dark "Pay by" box to its right, and the "Payment options" section across the lower part of the page. The whole bill exists to ask for this payment. The value starts with one of the contract's eight action words, and the experiments score that word.

### due_date

**2026-11-11.** Page 1, the dark box on the right of the three boxes under the address, "Pay by 11 Nov 2026". The date is not printed anywhere else on the bill. The pay-by date is the date the payment is due. The page writes the date as day, short month name and year. The answer key writes it as year, month, day.

### amount

**\$234.56.** Page 1, the dark box in the middle of the three boxes under the address, "Amount to pay \$234.56". It is also the last line of the summary inside the same box, "Total charges \$234.56". It is the whole amount she has to pay. The summary shows how it adds up. The payments received, printed as "Payments received -\$231.56", were \$3.86 more than her previous bill of \$227.70. That left her a credit of \$3.86, printed as "Balance \$3.86CR". The credit is taken off this bill's current charges of \$238.42.

### reference

**40740 95528.** Page 1, the light blue box on the left under the address, "Account number 40740 95528". The same number is printed in the paystead block at the bottom right of page 1 ("Your account number: 40740 95528"). The contract asks for the reference, account or customer number the person must quote, and the number this bill identifies her account by is the account number.

### identifiers

Every number the letter prints that identifies her, something she holds, or this matter, each under the label printed beside it, written as printed. The first list is the numbers a reading must find; the reference is one of them, and a reading that gives any of them as the reference is right. The experiments score whether every number in the first list is found.

# 06 · Parking infringement notice

Sample `SYN-0006`, printed in small type at the bottom centre of the page, under the barcode. One page, printed on a long narrow strip of paper like a receipt. A parking infringement notice from a fictional council, Calderfield City Council, about a car that was parked for longer than indicated in Marlow Street, Calderfield, on 23 June 2026. It names no person. It is issued to "THE OWNER". The answer key is [`ground-truth.json`](ground-truth.json).

## Where it comes from

This notice did not follow any official sample or annotated example, because none could be found. Four Victorian councils were checked, and none of them publishes a blank parking infringement notice. The Attorney-General's Guidelines for enforcement agencies were read in full, and they contain no sample notice and no example wording. A notice that a real person received was never an option, because the rules the synthetic data was made under do not allow a real person's document as a source.

So the notice was built up from what Victorian law says an infringement notice must contain, and from official government tables and forms:

- **Infringements Regulations 2026, regulation 13** (Victorian Legislation). The list of everything a Victorian infringement notice must show: a statement that it is an infringement notice, the notice date, the council's name, the notice's own number, the issuing officer's number, the date, time and place of the offence, the rule broken with a short description, the penalty, how to pay, the due date, what happens if the penalty is not paid, internal review, payment plans, where to get more information, legal help, help for people under 18, and financial advice. Most blocks on the notice exist because a part of this regulation requires them. Regulation 13(1)(d) lets a parking notice be issued to "the Owner" with no name. Regulation 13(2) requires a parking notice to show the car's registration number. These regulations started on 6 June 2026, and this notice is dated 23 June 2026. <https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/statutory-rules/infringements-regulations-2026/001>
- **Infringements Act 2006, version 065** (Victorian Legislation). Section 12(1)(c) allows a notice to be served by placing it on the vehicle. Section 14 says the time to pay must be at least 21 days after the notice is served. Section 12(3) makes a notice with less time than that invalid. Section 13(b) requires the notice to say the matter can be heard in court. Sections 22 and 46 cover internal review and payment plans. <https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/acts/infringements-act-2006/065>
- **Fees and Penalties 2025 to 2026** (Department of Transport and Planning, Victoria). The official spreadsheet of offence codes and penalties. Its row for code 701 gave the offence description "Parking for longer than indicated", the rule number 205(1), and a penalty of 0.2 penalty units. The same row prints that penalty as \$41. The table also says penalties are rounded to the nearest dollar. <https://transport.vic.gov.au/road-and-active-transport/registration-and-licensing/registration-and-licensing-fees/fines-and-fees>
- **Application for Internal Review (Infringements Only), form INF6.0.28 V6** (Fines Victoria). The official blank review form. It gave the five short names of the review grounds listed under "REVIEW OF THIS NOTICE". It also gave the sentence saying only one review may be made for each notice, with its exception for Person Unaware of Fine. The form has 10 boxes for the infringement notice number. The number on this notice has 10 digits to match. <https://online.fines.vic.gov.au/-/media/Files/Downloadable-applications/Application-for-Internal-Review.pdf>
- **Attorney-General's Guidelines to the Infringements Act 2006, for Enforcement Agencies** (Department of Justice and Community Safety, Victoria, October 2022). Section 2.1.4 lists what an issuing officer must check, including a person's date of birth. A date of birth is not in the regulation 13 list, so this notice does not print one. <https://www.justice.vic.gov.au/justice-system/attorney-generals-guidelines-to-the-infringements-act-2006/for-Enforcement-Agencies>
- **Fines Reform Act 2014, version 033** (Victorian Legislation). The regulations require the section on what happens if you do not act to name this Act as well as the Infringements Act 2006. It also sets out payment arrangements. <https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/in-force/acts/fines-reform-act-2014/033>
- **Road Safety Act 1986, section 87** (AustLII). Section 87(4) lets a council set its own parking penalty only if it is not more than 0.5 penalty unit and not more than the penalty in the regulations. This supported taking the penalty straight from the official table. <https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/rsa1986125/s87.html>
- **Understand your parking fine** (City of Boroondara). This council page says the offence time and the issue time on a parking fine can be different. It gave the separate "Time of offence" and "Issue time" lines. It also says parking officers do not legally need to record a car's make, colour or body type. <https://www.boroondara.vic.gov.au/services/streets-roads-and-parking/parking-fines/understand-your-parking-fine>
- **Parking fines** (City of Melbourne). This council page says the council gives 28 days to pay a parking fine. <https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/parking-fines>
- **Parking fines** (Merri-bek City Council). This council page also gives 28 days to pay. It showed that one of its infringement number formats has 10 digits starting with 7. <https://www.merri-bek.vic.gov.au/parkingfines>
- **Infringement Notice** (Fines Victoria). This page lists the options a person has after receiving an infringement notice. It says a council fine is dealt with by the council at this first stage. <https://online.fines.vic.gov.au/Infringement-fines/Infringement-Notice>

Made up, not taken from any source: the names Calderfield City Council and State Infringements Registry, the street and suburb, the infringement number, the registration number, the issuing officer number, the BPAY biller code and reference, every phone number, the PO Box, the web address, and the exact times of the offence and of issue. The choice of 28 days to pay, which sets the pay-by date, was made by the project, because the law only says at least 21 days. The "Vehicle" line is printed because councils commonly print it, and no rule requires it. The layout is made up: the order of the blocks, the label wording, which two lines are printed large, the dark "OFFENCE DETAILS" bar, and the solid and dashed lines. The receipt shape is made up: no source says a notice left on a car is printed on a thermal receipt, and the 80 mm paper width and the paper and ink colours were chosen by the project. The barcode is only a picture and encodes nothing. The wording and order of the six "YOUR OPTIONS" bullets are made up, although each option has a basis in the law or on council websites. In "HELP", the regulation names real organisations, and this notice describes them instead, as "the State legal aid commission" and "a free financial counselling service". The "INTERPRETER" section gives a made-up council phone line instead of a real interpreter service.

## The six fields

### document_type

**Parking infringement notice.** Page 1, top centre, under the council name, "INFRINGEMENT NOTICE" in large type and "Parking Infringement Notice" under it. The small grey text under it says "This is an infringement notice served under the Infringements Act 2006 and the Road Safety Act 1986." The answer key stores it as `parking_infringement_notice`. This field is free text and the experiments do not score it.

### issuer

**Calderfield City Council.** Page 1, top centre, the first line reads "CALDERFIELD CITY COUNCIL", with "PARKING SERVICES" under it. The name is printed again in normal case in the "INTERPRETER" section: "call Calderfield City Council on (03) 5550 3968." The council's web address, "www.calderfield-city-council.example/fines", is printed under "HOW TO PAY" and again under "ENQUIRIES". Both of those sections also give the council's PO Box: "PO Box 26, Calderfield 3999" under "HOW TO PAY" ("By post") and "PO Box 26, Calderfield VIC 3999" under "ENQUIRIES" ("Post"). It is the council that issued the notice.

### action_required

**Pay Calderfield City Council.** Page 1, the "PENALTY AMOUNT" and "PAY BY" lines below the offence details, and the "HOW TO PAY" section under them. The first bullet under "YOUR OPTIONS" says "Pay the penalty in full by the due date shown above." The "YOUR OPTIONS" section lists six things the owner can do, and paying is the first. The "WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DO NOT ACT" section says: "If the penalty is not paid, or you do not take another action available to you, by the due date shown on this notice, a penalty reminder notice may be issued and a fee will be added." The value starts with one of the contract's eight action words, and the experiments score that word.

### due_date

**2026-07-21.** Page 1, below the offence details, the "PAY BY" line, with "21/07/2026" in large type at the right. The date is printed only once. The first bullet under "YOUR OPTIONS" points back to it as "the due date shown above". The "WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DO NOT ACT" section calls it "the due date shown on this notice". The pay-by date is the date the penalty must be paid by. The page writes the date day first, the Australian way. The answer key writes it as year, month, day.

### amount

**\$41.00.** Page 1, below the offence details, the "PENALTY AMOUNT" line, with "\$41.00" in large type at the right. The amount is printed only once. It is the penalty for the offence the notice describes, "Parking for longer than indicated". The first bullet under "YOUR OPTIONS" asks for the penalty to be paid in full.

### reference

**7717145263.** Page 1, the block under the header, first line, "Infringement no. 7717145263". The same number is printed at the bottom of the page under the barcode, in small, widely spaced digits. The note under "HOW TO PAY" says "Quote the infringement number with every payment." The contract asks for the reference, account or customer number the person must quote, and this notice asks for the infringement number to be quoted.

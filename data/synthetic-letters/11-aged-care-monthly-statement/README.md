# 11 · Aged care monthly statement

Sample `SYN-0018`, printed in small type at the bottom left of each page. Eleven pages. A Support at Home monthly statement for July 2026 from a fictional aged care provider, Thornhurst Health, to Mrs M A Wilson. The answer key is [`ground-truth.json`](ground-truth.json).

## Where it comes from

This statement followed an official sample document. The Department of Health, Disability and Ageing publishes a Support at Home monthly statement template, which is an example statement for a made-up person. This letter copies that template page by page. It has the same 11 pages in the same order, the same tables and the same wording.

The parts of the template that carry the government's identity were removed and not replaced. These are the Commonwealth crest, the words "Australian Government", the department name, the photograph and the Support at Home logo. The template's brand colours were removed as well. They were replaced with teal and grey colours that are not from any source. The names of the government program and its parts are real and printed as they are: Support at Home, the Restorative Care Pathway, and the Assistive Technology and Home Modifications (AT-HM) scheme. The real organisation names and real phone numbers on the template's last page were replaced with a plain description of each service and a fictional number.

- **Support at Home monthly statement template, October 2025** (Department of Health, Disability and Ageing). The layout of all 11 pages is copied from this template. It gave the order of the sections, how every table is built, and the exact labels and fixed sentences, including the "This is not an invoice" box on page 1. It also gave the prices for most services, such as \$100.00 an hour for help with self-care and \$185.00 an hour for a physiotherapist. It gave the sentences on pages 5 and 7 that say when the assistive technology and home modification funding expires. <https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-10/support-at-home-monthly-statement-template.pdf>
- **Monthly statement guide for older people, October 2025** (Department of Health, Disability and Ageing). The official guide that explains each part of the statement to the person who receives it. It shows which parts the law requires and which parts the template adds on its own. The service type summary and the "This is not an invoice" box are template additions, not legal requirements. This decided which parts of the template could be left out. <https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-10/monthly-statement-guide-for-older-people.pdf>
- **Support at Home program manual, version 4.2, December 2025** (Department of Health, Disability and Ageing). Chapter 17 lists what a monthly statement must contain. Section 9.4 gives the three kinds of contribution rate. A worked example in section 9.6.1 shows a provider sending the monthly statement and the invoice as two separate documents. <https://www.health.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-12/support-at-home-program-manual-a-guide-for-registered-providers.pdf>
- **Aged Care Rules 2025, section 155-40** (Commonwealth of Australia). The legal list of what a monthly statement must contain. It also says a statement must be sent even in a month with no services. It says the statement must arrive by the last day of the following month. <https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L01173/latest/downloads>
- **Aged Care Act 2024, section 155** (Commonwealth of Australia). The section that makes giving and explaining this information a condition of a provider's registration. <https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2024A00104/latest/text>
- **Funding classifications for Support at Home** (Department of Health, Disability and Ageing). The quarterly budget of \$5,634.20 on page 2. It is the Classification 3 amount from 1 July 2026. <https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/support-at-home/funding-for-support-at-home/funding-classifications-for-support-at-home>
- **Monthly statements for Support at Home services** (Department of Health, Disability and Ageing). The last paragraph on page 11. It tells her she can raise concerns with the aged care regulator, and that she has the right to the information on the statement. <https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/support-at-home/managing-support-at-home-services/monthly-statements-for-support-at-home-services>
- **Centrepay Procedural Guide, version 6, March 2024** (Services Australia). Used only to explain why the statement has no payment section. The guide allows Centrepay wording on a bill only inside its "How to Pay" section. The template has no "How to Pay" section, because payment belongs on the separate invoice. <https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/centrepay-procedural-guide>

Made up, not taken from any source: the provider Thornhurst Health, the pharmacy Ledworth Central Pharmacy, Mrs Wilson and her address, her My Aged Care ID number, the Provider ID, the provider's phone number and email address, the advocacy line and translating line numbers, which services and items she received and on which dates, the \$349.84 of unspent funds carried over from June, the choice to put her on the high tier of assistive technology and home modification funding, and the teal and grey colours. Which contribution rate applies to each service was worked out from the template's own examples, not looked up in a table. Three layout choices are also ours. The phone number and email on page 1 are stacked, where the template puts them side by side. The address has no "Australia" line. Two wrong years printed in the template were not copied.

## The six fields

### document_type

**Aged care monthly statement.** Page 1, under the dark header band, the large title reads "Support at Home monthly statement". Pages 2 to 11 each have "Monthly statement" as the heading at the top left. Page 1 says "This statement includes information relating to the services you received in the previous calendar month." Page 11 speaks of "your aged care, including Support at Home costs and fees". The answer key stores it as `aged_care_monthly_statement`. This field is free text and the experiments do not score it.

### issuer

**Thornhurst Health.** Page 1, top left, the dark header band reads "Thornhurst Health", with "HOME AND COMMUNITY SERVICES" and "Registered Support at Home provider" under it. The same name is printed at the top right of pages 2 to 11, above "Home and Community Services". The contact email on page 1 and page 11 is `enquiries@thornhurst-health.example`. The header line "Registered Support at Home provider" says Thornhurst Health is her provider. The box on page 1 asks her to discuss any concerns "with your provider", and gives Thornhurst Health's phone number and email under it. Page 11 says "Please call or email us" and gives the same phone number and email.

### action_required

**No action.** Page 1, in the box under her address, the bold first line begins "This is not an invoice" and ends "no payment is required." The same box says "This statement includes information relating to the services you received in the previous calendar month." Page 4, under "Support at Home unspent funds", the first sentence is "This information is for your reference only and no action is required from you." The statement reports her budgets and the services already delivered. Page 1 says no payment is required. The box on page 1 also says "Please review this statement and discuss any concerns with your provider." This invites her to contact her provider if something looks wrong. It does not set a task or a date. The page 4 sentence is about her unspent funds. The value starts with one of the contract's eight action words, and the experiments score that word.

### due_date

**Not applicable.** No page of the statement has a pay by date or a due date. Page 1, in the box under her address, the bold first line begins "This is not an invoice" and ends "no payment is required." The statement also prints the dates when her funding ends. Page 5, in the italic line under the account summary table, says assistive technology short-term funding is "to expire 15 November 2026". Page 7, in the italic line under the account summary table, says home modifications funding is "to expire 15 November 2026". Page 9, under "Restorative care summary", says "Funding to end September 27, 2026." The same two dates are printed under the "Remaining budget" bars in the charts on page 1. Page 4, about her unspent funds, says "no action is required from you." The statement asks her to do nothing, so there is no date by which she must do it.

### amount

**No payment required.** Page 1, in the box under her address, the bold first line begins "This is not an invoice" and ends "no payment is required." The last sentence in the same box, just above the phone number, is "You will receive a separate invoice relating to payment of participant contributions." None of the 11 pages has a payment section, an amount due, payment methods or a payment slip. The statement says itself that it asks for no payment. It says her contributions will be billed on a different document.

### reference

**AC87542.** Page 1, the details block on the right beside her address, second row, "My Aged Care ID No:". It is printed only on page 1. The first row of the same block is "Client Name:" with her full name, Margaret Anne Wilson, and this number is the ID number given for her. The contract asks for the reference, account or customer number the person must quote, and the number this statement identifies her by is her My Aged Care ID number.

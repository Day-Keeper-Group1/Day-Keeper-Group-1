# Dental callback: the Voice demonstration recording

`recording.wav` is this conversation, about 36 seconds, under the Voice page's
45-second limit. Upload it on the Voice page to show a conversation becoming
tasks. It has no prepared answer: it is for showing a live extraction with
`AI_VOICE_EXTRACTION_PROVIDER=azure`, not for scoring.

It was made with Windows speech synthesis, Sarah in the Zira voice and Margaret
in the Catherine voice, so no real person's voice is in it. Margaret says yes to
each request, because a request nobody accepts is marked `uncertain` and is not
shown. The year is spoken as words: the browser's transcription model misheard
"twenty twenty-six".

On the deployed site on 15 October 2026 it gave the same two commitments three
times out of three: call Bayside Dental to confirm, due 2026-11-06, and bring
the Medicare card to the check-up, due 2026-11-12 at 10:00. Both dates fall
after the final presentation on 29 October.

**Sarah:** Hello Margaret, this is Sarah from Bayside Dental. Your check-up is on Thursday, the twelfth of November, two thousand and twenty-six, at ten in the morning. Could you call us back by Friday, the sixth of November, to confirm?

**Margaret:** Yes, I will call you back by Friday, the sixth of November, two thousand and twenty-six.

**Sarah:** Lovely. Please bring your Medicare card.

**Margaret:** I will bring my Medicare card to the check-up. Goodbye.

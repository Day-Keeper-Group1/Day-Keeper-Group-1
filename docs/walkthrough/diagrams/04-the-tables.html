The twelve tables, and which step writes each

STEP 1
She photographs the letter
POST /api/documents
STEP 2
The system reads it
GET /api/home  (polled)
STEP 3  ·  WRITES NOTHING
She checks it
GET /api/documents/:id
STEP 4
Her yes writes the rows
POST /api/documents/:id/confirm
STEP 5
A reminder rings
the clock, at nine
STEP 6
She ticks it
POST /api/tasks/:id/complete

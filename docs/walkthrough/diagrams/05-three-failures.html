The wording differs by cause because the causes are genuinely different, and this reader assumes every error is hers. The one that really is ours says so first. But the follow up is one thing everywhere: the row goes away, and where photographing again could change the answer, the camera opens. There is no special mode afterwards. What she posts next is an ordinary pile, and nothing assumes it is that letter, or only that letter, or any letter at all.

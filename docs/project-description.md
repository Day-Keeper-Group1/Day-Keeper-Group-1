**Company**: Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology
**Project ID**: P000473SE
**Project Title**: DayKeeper (AI-Powered Life Management System)
**Working format**: Hybrid
**Multiple teams**: Yes

## Project Description

> Many individuals, particularly those in vulnerable groups, face challenges in managing daily communications and obligations across various channels, such as letters, emails, and phone calls. This often results in missed deadlines and financial penalties, increased anxiety and cognitive overload, fragmented information and lack of coordination, as well as reduced independence and quality of life.
>
> Module 1: Physical Document Intelligence, Smart scanning of letters and documents, OCR and AI-based extraction of key information.
> Module 2: Email Intelligence, Automated email monitoring, AI extraction of tasks, deadlines, and contacts.
> Module 3: Voice & Conversation Intelligence, Call transcription (with consent), Extraction of commitments and follow-ups.
> Module 4: Unified Archive & Action Engine, Central data repository, Task generation and reminders, Automated or assisted execution of actions.
>
> Our goal is to develop a unified AI-powered responsive web application that functions seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers. This application will capture, understand, organise, and respond to all incoming communications. By doing so, it will enable users to manage their daily obligations with minimal effort.

## Project Deliverables

> For the first version of the prototype, we aim to implement core functionalities such as smart scanning of letters and documents, OCR, and AI-based extraction of key information. The system should also be able to analyse the extracted content, generate notifications, and define required action items. In addition, we would develop a user registration system and an admin dashboard (Modules 1 and 4).
>
> If time permits, the next phase could include additional features such as email integration for accessing and analysing emails, as well as voice recognition capabilities (Modules 2 and 3).

DayKeeper 1.0.1
===============

Release date: 19 October 2026
Git tag:      v1.0.1
Repository:   https://github.com/Day-Keeper-Group1/Day-Keeper-Group-1
Deployed at:  https://preview--daykeeper-group1.netlify.app

DayKeeper reads the letters, emails and phone calls an older person receives,
finds what she has to do and by when, and, only after she has checked and
confirmed it, puts the task on her calendar with reminders.
RMIT COSC2648, project P000473SE, Group 1.


What this release does
----------------------

Letters
- Photograph a letter, every page of it, or upload a PDF; a PDF is turned into
  one image per page in the browser.
- The letter is read by two model readings that must agree, with a third
  reading to break a tie (a voting workflow on Azure OpenAI). A due date or
  amount the readings cannot settle is never guessed.
- "Check what we found" shows what the letter says, the number to quote, and
  exactly which reminders and calendar entry will be created. Nothing is saved
  until she confirms. The photos stay with the letter and open with zoom.

Tasks, calendar and reminders
- Home: today, overdue, upcoming, and what is waiting for her check.
- Calendar by month or week; a day shows each task with every detail read.
- Task list with filters and search; ticking a task done stops its reminders.
- Reminders are days marked on Home, 7, 3 and 1 days before the due date;
  the calendar marks the due date. Nothing is sent by email or text.

Email
- Connect a Gmail mailbox (Google OAuth, read only). The last 30 days of the
  inbox are listed, at most 20 messages; only the emails she chooses are read.
- An email becomes a task through the same check as a letter; a field the
  model could not settle, such as a date without a year, is highlighted for
  her to correct next to the original email.

Voice
- Record a short conversation, up to 45 seconds, or upload a recording.
- It is transcribed in the browser (whisper-tiny.en); the audio never leaves
  the device. The transcript is sent to Azure OpenAI to find what she agreed to.
- Each commitment is checked with the exact words it came from highlighted,
  and becomes a task only when she confirms it.

Settings
- Profile, sign out of this device, Gmail connection; Settings opens from
  the account menu.
- Customisation: which sections Home shows and in what order, how the
  calendar opens, how the task list is sorted and grouped.
- Accessibility: three text sizes, palettes for four kinds of colour vision,
  extra contrast and reduced motion.

Accounts and operations
- Register and sign in with email and password; sessions last 30 days.
- Every pull request is built and tested, and gets its own preview site.
- Every merge to main applies database migrations, deploys, and checks
  /api/health. A failed run on main opens a GitHub issue.
- A "Demo accounts" button in GitHub Actions refreshes the demonstration
  account or empties one @example.com account.
- The school's Azure key has a daily ceiling of 200 model calls for the whole
  site.
- Once a day a GitHub Actions workflow calls /api/health, so the free
  Supabase database is not paused for inactivity.

Known issues (open Jira bugs)
-----------------------------

KAN-65  A reading can list things that are not identifiers (for example a
        date of birth or the sender's own payment code), and they reach the
        screen.


Limits of this deployment (not bugs)
------------------------------------

- The Google app behind Gmail is in Testing: only the test mailbox listed in
  readme.txt can connect, and its authorisation lasts 7 days. Reconnect it on
  the Email page before a demonstration.
- Free plans: Supabase pauses a project that gets too few database queries in
  a week. A GitHub Actions workflow asks the site's database a question every
  day to keep it running; if it is paused anyway, restore it from the Supabase
  dashboard. Netlify stops a request at 30 seconds.
- After 200 model calls in a day, new readings wait until midnight in
  Melbourne.
- Voice works in English only, and the recording must be 45 seconds or less.
